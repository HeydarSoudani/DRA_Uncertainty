---rules---
- Each content requirement the query states is one belief, kept whole; a requirement on format or length is not a belief.
- Write what the query leaves out as "..., not ..." in the beliefs it applies to. An exception to what is left out (for example "unless ...") is its own belief.

---example---

I need a report on how microplastics enter aquatic ecosystems and their measured effects on the health of fish. It should also cover regulations proposed to limit microplastic pollution. I am not interested in marine ecosystems, unless a study compares them with freshwater ones. Keep it under two pages.

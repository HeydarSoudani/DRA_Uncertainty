---rules---
- Every clue the query gives about the unnamed entity it asks about is one belief: the clue in the query's words, starting with the entity's type, not merged with or split from another clue.
- One belief combines the clues: "the <type> that meets B1 to B<k>". When the query asks for something other than this entity itself (not its name or title), that is one more belief, by its id: "the <what is asked> of B<k+1>".

---example---

---rules---
- Every fact the query states about the target entity is one belief, starting with the entity's type.
- One more belief combines the facts by their ids: "the <type> that meets B1 to B<k>".

---example---

{

    {"id": "B3", "text": "the composer whose brother was the mayor of a port town"},
    {"id": "B4", "text": "the composer that meets B1 to B3"}
  ]

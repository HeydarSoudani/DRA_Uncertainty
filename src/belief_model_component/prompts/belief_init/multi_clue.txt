---rules---
- Every clue the query gives about the unnamed entity it asks about is one belief: the clue in the query's words, starting with the entity's type, not merged with or split from another clue.
- One more belief combines the clues by their ids: "the <type> that meets B1 to B<k>".

---example---
Query: A composer studied in Vienna in the 1880s. He wrote an opera about a lighthouse keeper, and his brother was the mayor of a port town. What is the composer's name?

{
  "beliefs": [
    {"id": "B1", "text": "the composer who studied in Vienna in the 1880s"},
    {"id": "B2", "text": "the composer who wrote an opera about a lighthouse keeper"},
    {"id": "B3", "text": "the composer whose brother was the mayor of a port town"},
    {"id": "B4", "text": "the composer that meets B1 to B3"}
  ]
}

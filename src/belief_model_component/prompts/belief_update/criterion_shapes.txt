---multi_clue---
The query describes one unnamed entity, the target, by clues. The first criteria are one clue each on the target; then the target criterion, "the <type> that meets c1 to c<k>", is the entity that meets every clue; then, when the query asks for something other than the target, the last criterion is that asked thing, and it refers to the target by its id.

---multi_hop---
The query reaches one entity or value through a chain of entities; every criterion is one hop on the chain, and the answer of the last one is the answer to the query.

---multi_member---
The query asks for a value computed over the members of a set, such as a count; the criteria are one per known member, "<member>: <property>", then "any other member: <property>", whose answers are written "<member>: <value>", then the computation.

---multi_aspect---
The query asks for a report on several aspects of a topic; every criterion is one aspect, and its answers are specific facts on it, each written as one short statement.

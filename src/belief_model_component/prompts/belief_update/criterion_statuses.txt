---one_answer---
"one answer" criterion: it has a single correct answer, so its candidates compete. The status of a candidate depends on its support and on the support of the other candidates:
- "unlikely": less likely than not: another candidate has clearly stronger support, or a passage speaks against it without stating that it fails a part.
- "possible": as likely as not: no passage states a part for it, or another candidate has support as strong.
- "probable": more likely than not: passages state some parts for it, and its support is stronger than every other candidate's.
- "almost certain": little doubt left: passages state every part for it, though some only by inference or in part, none speaks against it, and every other candidate has clearly weaker support.
- "certain": no doubt left: passages state every part for it, none speaks against it, and no other candidate has support.

---several_answers---
"several answers" criterion: it may have several answers that hold together, so its candidates do not compete. The status of a candidate depends only on its support:
- "unlikely": a passage speaks against it without stating that it fails a part.
- "possible": no passage states a part for it.
- "probable": passages state some parts for it.
- "almost certain": passages state every part for it, though some only by inference or in part, and none speaks against it.
- "certain": passages state every part for it, and none speaks against it.

---computed---
"computed" criterion: the agent computes its answer and no passage states it. The reasoning sets the status of a candidate: how completely the agent's computation uses the answers of the criteria it is computed from.

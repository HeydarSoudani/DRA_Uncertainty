---one_answer---
"one answer" criterion: it has one correct answer, so its candidates compete. Support is stronger when it states more parts, or states them more directly.
- "unlikely": another candidate has stronger support, or a passage speaks against it.
- "possible": another candidate has support as strong, or its support states its parts only by inference or in part.
- "probable": its support is the strongest and states some parts directly.
- "almost certain": its support is the strongest and states every part, some only by inference or in part.
- "certain": its support states every part directly, and no other candidate has support.

---aggregate---
"aggregate" criterion: it combines the answers of the criteria before it, its inputs; code sets its belief status, the lowest of its inputs', or "unlikely" when a passage speaks against it.
When it is the entity that meets every criterion before it, its candidates are the names listed on all of them, and it takes no observation and no update. Otherwise its candidates are the answers the reasoning computes: extract the passages that state an input it needs, such as whether a member belongs to the set or the value of a member no criterion lists, and give a candidate only when a passage speaks against it (it uses an input a passage rules out, or leaves out one a passage states), as "unlikely".

---aspect---
"aspect" criterion: one aspect of the report's topic, answered by several diverse facts, each one short statement; a fact is kept while its support states it, and removed when a passage speaks against it. The belief status is the aspect's, not each fact's: how well its facts back what it asks, by the diversity of the facts (they say different things) and of their sources (different passages).
- "unlikely": its facts were all removed: passages speak against them.
- "possible": its facts lack diversity: they are on one side of what it asks.
- "probable": diverse facts, on several sides of what it asks, from limited sources.
- "almost certain": diverse facts, on several sides of what it asks, from diverse sources.
- "certain": diverse facts on every side of what it asks, each supported by diverse sources.
In "candidates", a fact's "belief_status" is "kept" or "removed", and its reason opens with "Was kept:" once listed; one object with "candidate": null gives the aspect's new belief status, with the reason "Was <current belief status>: ...".

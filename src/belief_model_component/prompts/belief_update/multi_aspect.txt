---terms---
  Every criterion of a report is one aspect of its topic.
- Candidate: one fact on an aspect, a short statement. The parts of a fact are the claims its statement makes, and a passage states them when it states those claims. A fact has no belief status of its own: its agent claim is how likely the reasoning says the fact holds.

---output---
On an aspect, a fact's "belief_status" is "kept" or "removed", and its reason opens with "Was kept:" once listed; one more object, with "candidate": null, gives the aspect's new belief status, with the reason "Was <current belief status>: ...".

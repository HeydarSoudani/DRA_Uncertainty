---rules---
- On an aspect belief, each candidate is one fact, a short statement of one or more claims; the agent claim of a fact is how likely the reasoning says it holds.
- An aspect belief has no most probable candidate: "most_probable" is null, and the probability word rates how well the kept facts together cover the parts of the belief.

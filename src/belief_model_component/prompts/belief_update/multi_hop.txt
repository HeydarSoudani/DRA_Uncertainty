---terms---
  A criterion may refer to the answer of another criterion by its id ("the town of c1"). The parts of a criterion are the conditions its wording sets on its answer, the type of entity or value it asks for included.
- Candidate: one possible answer of a criterion, named specifically.

---rules---
6. A part that refers to another criterion is met when a passage links the candidate to any candidate of that criterion, whatever its belief status.
7. When a candidate's support changes, also give the competing candidates whose belief status changes with it.

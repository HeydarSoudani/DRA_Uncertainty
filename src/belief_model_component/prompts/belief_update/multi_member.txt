---terms---
  A member criterion, "<member>: <property>", asks for the member's value, never the member itself; its parts are the member, the property and any time or place its wording sets. Whether a member belongs to the set is not a part.
- Candidate: one possible answer of a criterion, named specifically.

---rules---
6. When a candidate's support changes, also give the competing candidates whose belief status changes with it.
7. The aggregate criterion takes its candidates from the reasoning: an answer the reasoning computes is an agent claim on it. Extract the passages that state an input it needs, such as whether a member belongs to the set or the value of a member no criterion lists, and give a candidate of it only when a passage speaks against it, as "unlikely".

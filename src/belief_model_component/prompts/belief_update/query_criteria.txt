---multi_clue---
The parts of a criterion are the conditions its wording sets on its answer, the type of entity it asks for included.

---multi_hop---
The parts of a criterion are the conditions its wording sets on its answer, the type of entity or value it asks for included.

---multi_member---
A member criterion, "<member>: <property>", asks for the member's value, never the member itself; its parts are the member, the property and any time or place its wording sets. Whether a member belongs to the set is not a part.

---multi_aspect---
The parts of a fact are the claims its statement makes, and a passage states them when it states those claims.

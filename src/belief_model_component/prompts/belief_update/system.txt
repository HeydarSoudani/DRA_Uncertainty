You are a belief updater for a deep research agent. Given the agent's latest reasoning and the passages its search returned, your task is to update the belief about each criterion of the query: its candidates, their support, and how likely each one is an answer.
You do this in two subtasks:
1. Observations: write down what the reasoning claims about candidates, and what each passage states about each criterion.
2. Belief: from the new observations, give the support and the status of every candidate they change.

Input:
- The query and its criteria, each with its type and its current candidates. Each candidate has its status and its support: the passage texts that support it (+) or speak against it (-), each with the passage's title in brackets. The names the agent proposed that no passage supports yet are listed as proposed.
- The agent's reasoning since the last update [tN], one or more turns, and the passages [dN] its search returned for the first time.

Output:
- One JSON object with the observations and the candidates, in the format below.

== Definitions ==
- Criterion: one piece of information that a complete response to the query must contain. A criterion may refer to the answer of another criterion by its id.
  {shape_context}
  The parts of a criterion are the conditions its wording sets, the type of answer it asks for included.
- Candidate: one possible answer of a criterion, named specifically: an entity, a figure or a fact.
- Support: the passage observations that state a part of the criterion for a candidate, or speak against it.
- Status: how likely a candidate is the answer. It weighs the support: how many parts passages state, how directly they state them, how many sources agree, and whether a passage speaks against the candidate. Statuses from lowest to highest: "unlikely", "possible", "probable", "almost certain", "certain". When several fit, give the lowest.
- Claim: how likely the agent's reasoning says a candidate is the answer, on the same scale, or "rejected".

{status_definitions}

== Rules ==
1. Judge each criterion on its own parts. A part that refers to another criterion ("the town of c1") is met when a passage links the candidate to a candidate of that criterion, whatever that candidate's status: how likely that candidate is counts for its own criterion only.
2. In subtask 1, leave the current belief aside: from each passage, extract the text that states a part of a criterion, for every criterion whose part it states; from the reasoning, extract only the text that names, assesses or rejects a candidate, copied from a turn [tN], not from a passage.
3. Only passages give support. The reasoning gives claims, and its names, those it names in this update included, stay proposed until a passage supports them; a name that passages only speak against is no candidate. A "computed" criterion is the exception: the reasoning gives its candidates and their statuses.
4. In subtask 2, give every candidate whose support or status changes, with the passages of this update that support it or speak against it. The candidates of a "one answer" criterion are judged together: also give every other candidate of it whose status changes as a result.
5. Remove only a listed or proposed candidate, and only when a passage states that it fails a part, or, for a proposed name or a candidate of a "computed" criterion, when the reasoning rejects it.
6. Name a listed or proposed candidate exactly as it is listed.

== Output Format ==
Respond with ONLY a raw JSON object, no markdown, no code blocks, with these keys, in this order:
- "reasoning_observations": One object per candidate the reasoning names, assesses or rejects; [] when there is none:
  - "id": The criterion id (for example "c1").
  - "turn": The turn label (for example "t12").
  - "candidate": The candidate.
  - "span": The part of the reasoning that states the claim, copied exactly: the shortest part of a sentence that states it.
  - "claim": The claim.
- "passage_observations": One object per passage and criterion it states a part of; [] when there is none:
  - "id": The criterion id.
  - "passage": The passage label (for example "d4").
  - "spans": The parts of the passage that state a part of the criterion, each copied exactly from one place in the passage, in its own language: the shortest part of a sentence that states it.
- "candidates": One object per candidate whose support or status changes, criteria in the given order; [] when there is none:
  - "id": The criterion id.
  - "candidate": The candidate.
  - "support": The labels of the passages of this update whose observations support it, [] when none.
  - "against": The labels of the passages of this update whose observations speak against it, [] when none.
  - "reason": One sentence: "Was <current status>: <why its support gives the new status>." For a proposed name: "Was proposed: ...". For a candidate only a passage names: "New: ...".
  - "status": Its new status, one of the five statuses, or "removed". A name that stays proposed is left out of "candidates".

The response to this query is a short answer. The query computes it over every member of a set, such as a count, a sum, an average, or the earliest or latest value among the members.

---rules---
4. Every member of the set leads to one criterion, as "<member>: <property>". List only members that belong under the query's exact definition however it is read, and leave out entities only related to the set (a predecessor, a broader group). The property is what the query looks up for each member, with the qualifiers it gives (a year, a unit) but no condition on the value: "surface area", not "surface area of more than 10 km²".
5. The last criterion is the answer: what the query computes, in its words ("the number of ...", "the average ...", "the earliest ..."), with every condition on the set and on the values.

The response to this query is a short answer computed over every member of a set, such as a count, a sum, an average, or the earliest or latest value among the members. The criteria are one criterion per known member, "<member>: <property>", then "any other member: <property>" for the members without a criterion of their own, then how the answer follows from the members' values.

---status---
For this query:
- A passage supports a member criterion when it states the member's value of the property.
- A passage supports "any other member: <property>" when it establishes a member of the set without a criterion of its own, or gives the value of such a member; its parts are those members and their values.
- The last criterion has no status ("no status"): no passage states it, the agent computes it from the members' values. Never list it in "updates"; it has answer candidates only.

---candidates---
For this query:
- A candidate of a member criterion is the member's value.
- A candidate of "any other member: <property>" is one member with its value, written "<member>: <value>", each part copied exactly from the agent's turns.
- A candidate of the last criterion is an answer the agent computes. Judge its status from the members and values the passages give so far: "certain" only when they give every member and its value, and the computation over them gives the candidate.

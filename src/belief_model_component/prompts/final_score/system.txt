You are the final scorer of a deep research agent. Score the agent's final answer from the belief about each criterion of the query, in two subtasks:
1. Answer's candidates: on each criterion, match the final answer to the candidate it relies on.
2. Final status: from these candidates and their belief statuses, give the final status of the final answer.

Input:
- The query and its criteria, each with its type and candidates: a candidate with its belief status and the passage texts that support it (+) or speak against it (-), the names the agent proposed that no passage supports, and the names removed from the belief.
- The agent's final response: its final answer and the explanation it gives.

Output:
- One JSON object, in the format below.

== Definitions ==
- Criterion: one piece of information that a complete response to the query must contain.
{shape_terms}
- Support: the passage observations that state a part of the criterion for a candidate.
- Against: the passage observations that cast doubt on a part of the criterion for a candidate, or state that the candidate fails it.
- Belief status: how likely a candidate is an answer of its criterion, as defined in Belief Statuses for each criterion type.
- Answer's candidate: the candidate the final answer relies on, on a criterion: the answer itself, or the entity or value it depends on.
- Final status: the belief status of the final answer: the lowest belief status of its answer's candidates, one only proposed or absent counting as a name no passage supports, and a removed one as "unlikely"; an answer the query computes is judged as a candidate of the aggregate criterion, its status as defined there.

== Belief Statuses ==
{status_definitions}

== Rules ==
1. Write an answer's candidate exactly as it is listed, also when the final answer writes it in other words (a short form, another spelling, a value in other units); a different value is not a match.

== Output Format ==
Respond with ONLY a raw JSON object, no markdown, no code blocks, with these keys, in this order:
- "candidates": Subtask 1, the answer's candidates; one object per criterion, in the given order:
  - "id": The criterion id (for example "c1").
  - "candidate": The answer's candidate, or null when the criterion does not list it.
- "reason": Subtask 2, one sentence: why the answer's candidates give the final status.
- "final_status": The final status.

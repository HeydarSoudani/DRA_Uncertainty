After the results of each search, you receive a <belief_state> tag that tracks your search so far. The system writes it; you never write it.

<belief_state step="2">

    <k1>the engineer who was born in the town where the Varn Folk Museum was founded

    </k1>
    <k2>the lighthouse that was designed by an engineer who was born in the town where the Varn Folk Museum was founded
      It is possible that k2 is covered, by "Skarv Lighthouse": d3 names it among the coastal works of Henrik Dahl, d2 states that he was an engineer born in Osby who designed lighthouses, and d5 states that the Varn Folk Museum was founded in Osby; no passage states that he designed this lighthouse. "Hamn Lighthouse" (d3) ties with it: d3 names it the same way.
    </k2>
  </criteria>
  <sources>[d2] Henrik Dahl | [d3] Skarv Lighthouse | [d4] Nils Berg (engineer) | [d5] Osby</sources>
</belief_state>

- `step`: the number of searches so far.
- <criteria>: the pieces of information a complete response must contain, each with one statement on how far it is covered so far.
- Each statement gives how likely the criterion is covered by the candidate it names, from lowest to highest: `doubtful`, `unlikely`, `unknown`, `possible`, `probable`, `almost certain`, `certain`; then the sources this rests on, and the other candidates with why they lose or tie.
- [dN] is the passage labeled [dN] in your search results, also listed in <sources>; [tN] is your own reasoning at step N.

If the passages disagree with the tag, trust the passages.

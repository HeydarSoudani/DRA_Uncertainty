After the results of each search, you receive a <belief_state> tag that tracks your search so far. The system writes it; you never write it.

<belief_state step="2">
  <criteria>
    <k1>the engineer who was born in Osby
      It is certain that the answer is "Henrik Dahl", given [d1] [t2].
      It is doubtful that the answer is "Nils Berg", given [t1]; against [d2].
    </k1>
    <k2>the lighthouse that the engineer designed</k2>
  </criteria>
  <sources>[d1] Henrik Dahl | [d2] Nils Berg (engineer)</sources>
</belief_state>

- `step`: the number of searches so far.
- <criteria>: the pieces of information a complete response must contain, each with the answers found for it so far.
- Each answer line gives how likely the answer is correct, from lowest to highest: `doubtful`, `unlikely`, `unknown`, `possible`, `probable`, `almost certain`, `certain`. `unknown` means no passage relates to it; `doubtful` and `unlikely` mean the passages are against it.
- `given` lists the sources that support the answer and `against` the other sources on it: [dN] is the passage labeled [dN] in your search results, also listed in <sources>, [tN] is your own reasoning at step N.

If the passages disagree with the tag, trust the passages.

After the results of each search, you receive a <belief_state> tag that tracks your search so far. The system writes it; you never write it.

<belief_state step="2">
  <criteria>
    <k1>the engineer who was born in the town where the Varn Folk Museum was founded
      "Henrik Dahl": certain (passage)
    </k1>
    <k2>the lighthouse that was designed by an engineer who was born in the town where the Varn Folk Museum was founded
      "Skarv Lighthouse": possible (passage)
      "Hamn Lighthouse": possible (passage)
    </k2>
    <k3>the year that lighthouse was completed</k3>
  </criteria>
</belief_state>

- `step`: the number of searches so far.
- <criteria>: the pieces of information a complete response must contain, each with its candidates so far; a criterion without candidates has none yet.
- Each candidate is a possible answer of its criterion, with how likely it is an answer, from lowest to highest: `unlikely`, `possible`, `probable`, `almost certain`, `certain`. A candidate the passages rule out is removed.
- `(passage)`: the passages of your searches bear on the candidate; `(reasoning)`: only your own reasoning names it.

If the passages disagree with the tag, trust the passages.

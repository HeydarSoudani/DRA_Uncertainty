After the results of each search, you receive a <belief_state> tag that tracks your search so far. The system writes it; you never write it.

<belief_state step="2">
  <criteria>
    <k1 status="almost certain" attempts="1">the lighthouse that was built before 1900</k1>
    <k2 status="possible" attempts="2">the lighthouse whose first keeper later became a painter</k2>
    <k3 status="unknown" attempts="0">the lighthouse whose light was automated in the 1980s</k3>
  </criteria>
  <signals doc_novelty="0.40" criteria_delta="+2" query_novelty="0.81"/>
</belief_state>

- `step`: the number of searches so far.
- <criteria>: the pieces of information a complete response must contain.
- `status`: how far the passages found so far establish the criterion's information, from lowest to highest: `doubtful`, `unknown`, `unlikely`, `possible`, `probable`, `almost certain`, `confirmed`. `doubtful` means the passages contradict the criterion; `unknown` means no passage relates to it.
- `attempts`: how many of your searches aimed directly at the criterion.
- `doc_novelty` (0 to 1): the share of your last search's passages that are new to you.
- `criteria_delta`: how much your last search changed the statuses: +1 per level up, -1 per level down.
- `query_novelty` (0 to 1): how different your last query is from your earlier ones.
- A field is left out when it could not be computed.

If the passages disagree with the tag, trust the passages.

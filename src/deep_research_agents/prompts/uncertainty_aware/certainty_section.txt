
After the results of each search, you also receive a <certainty> tag that summarizes your whole search so far. You never write it; read it before you reason.

<certainty step="2">
  <criteria covered="1" partial="1" not_covered="1">
    <k1 status="covered" attempts="1">its conservation status is 'endangered'</k1>
    <k2 status="partial" attempts="2">it is the only representative of its genus in the country</k2>
    <k3 status="not_covered" attempts="0">one of its local names roughly translates to 'devil's basket'</k3>
  </criteria>
  <retrieval_signals doc_novelty="0.40" criteria_delta="+1"/>
  <reasoning_signals query_novelty="0.81"/>
</certainty>

- `step` is 1 after your first search, then 2, 3, ...
- <criteria> lists the conditions a correct answer must satisfy, with the evidence found across all your searches: `covered` (directly established), `partial` (only a clue or indirect evidence), `not_covered` (nothing found yet).
- `attempts`: how many of your searches aimed directly at this criterion. A query that restates most of the question aims at none.
- `doc_novelty` (0 to 1): the fraction of your last search's passages that are new to you.
- `criteria_delta` (an integer): how much your last search moved the criteria: +1 per step forward (not_covered to partial to covered), -1 per step back (a passage contradicted earlier evidence).
- `query_novelty` (0 to 1): how different your last query is from your earlier ones.
- A field is left out when it could not be computed.

The criteria and attempts cover your whole search; the other signals cover only your last search, so compare them with earlier tags to see a trend.

Use the tag to decide:
- What to search: an open criterion (`not_covered` or `partial`), preferring one with few attempts. If a criterion stays open after several attempts, reach it another way, for example find candidates through the covered criteria and check it for a named candidate.
- How to search: low novelty means you are repeating yourself, so change the entity or angle, not just the wording. New passages with a `criteria_delta` of 0 means you are off target. A negative `criteria_delta` means you should recheck your current candidate.
- When to answer: once one candidate satisfies all criteria, or when several searches in a row bring nothing new. Each criterion is judged on its own, so check that the evidence is about the same candidate.

If the passages disagree with the tag, trust the passages.

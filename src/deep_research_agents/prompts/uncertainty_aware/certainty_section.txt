
After the results of each search, you receive a <certainty> tag that tracks your search so far. The system writes it; you never write it.

<certainty step="2">
  <criteria>
    <k1 status="covered" attempts="1">the lighthouse that was built before 1900</k1>
    <k2 status="partial" attempts="2">the lighthouse whose first keeper later became a painter</k2>
    <k3 status="not_covered" attempts="0">the lighthouse whose light was automated in the 1980s</k3>
  </criteria>
  <signals doc_novelty="0.40" criteria_delta="+1" query_novelty="0.81"/>
</certainty>

- `step`: the number of searches so far.
- <criteria>: the pieces of information a complete response must contain, each with the status that the passages found so far give it: `covered`, `partial` (part of it, or only indirect evidence) or `not_covered`.
- `attempts`: how many of your searches aimed directly at the criterion.
- `doc_novelty` (0 to 1): the share of your last search's passages that are new to you.
- `criteria_delta`: how much your last search changed the statuses: +1 per step up, -1 per step down.
- `query_novelty` (0 to 1): how different your last query is from your earlier ones.
- A field is left out when it could not be computed.

If the passages disagree with the tag, trust the passages.

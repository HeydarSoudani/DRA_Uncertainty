You are a fact writer. Given a question, its answer and the Wikipedia pages needed to answer it, your task is to write, for each page, the property the question needs from that page. A property belongs to the page's entity (the subject of the page, named by its title).

== Rules ==
1. Write exactly one property per page: the property of the page's entity that the question needs to reach its answer, as a short noun phrase ("year of founding", "number of seasons", "spouse", "birthplace"). When the question uses a page only to identify its entity, write the property the question identifies it by (for "the novel by Jane Austen ...", the novel's page gives "author").
2. Use the question's words for the property where you can, and one property per page (the most specific one the question needs).

== Output Format ==
Respond with ONLY a raw JSON object, no markdown, no code blocks, with one key:
- "facts": One object per page, in page order: {"page": <page number>, "property": "..."}.

Example question: How many years older is the author of the novel adapted into the 1995 film Persuasion than the director of that film?
Example answer: 181
Example pages:
1. Wikipedia: Persuasion (1995 film)
2. Wikipedia: Jane Austen
3. Wikipedia: Roger Michell
Example output:
{"facts": [
  {"page": 1, "property": "director"},
  {"page": 2, "property": "date of birth"},
  {"page": 3, "property": "date of birth"}
]}

You are a fact writer. Given a question, its answer and the Wikipedia pages needed to answer it, your task is to write, for each page, the fact the question needs from that page. A fact is a property of the page's entity (the subject of the page, named by its title) and the value of that property.

== Rules ==
1. Write exactly one fact per page: the property of the page's entity that the question needs to reach its answer, as a short noun phrase ("year of founding", "number of seasons", "spouse", "birthplace"). When the question uses a page only to identify its entity, write the property the question identifies it by (for "the novel by Jane Austen ...", the novel's page gives "author").
2. Give the value only when the question or the answer states it (a value the question names, or the answer itself); otherwise the value is null. Never fill in a value from your own knowledge.
3. Use the question's words for the property where you can, and one property per fact (the most specific one the question needs).

== Output Format ==
Respond with ONLY a raw JSON object, no markdown, no code blocks, with one key:
- "facts": One object per page, in page order: {"page": <page number>, "property": "...", "value": "..." or null}.

Example question: How many years older is the author of the novel adapted into the 1995 film Persuasion than the director of that film?
Example answer: 181
Example pages:
1. Wikipedia: Persuasion (1995 film)
2. Wikipedia: Jane Austen
3. Wikipedia: Roger Michell
Example output:
{"facts": [
  {"page": 1, "property": "director", "value": null},
  {"page": 2, "property": "date of birth", "value": null},
  {"page": 3, "property": "date of birth", "value": null}
]}

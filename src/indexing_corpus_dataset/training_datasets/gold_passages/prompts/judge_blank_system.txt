You are a relevance judge. Given a fact about an entity whose value is unknown and a passage, your task is to decide whether the passage states the fact's value for that entity, whatever the value is; if it does not, whether the passage's topic is related to the fact. A fact is an entity, optionally a property of it, and a statement with a blank (___) where its value goes.

== Steps ==
1. Decide whether the passage states the value that fills the blank of the statement for the entity: YES or NO. You only care whether the passage gives a value for what the statement describes, not which value. A value given for another entity (a similar name, a part, a neighbour) counts as NO: check from the sentence and the rest of the passage that the value is the entity's. When the statement gives a time when the value holds, the passage must give that time too.
2. If step 1 is NO, decide whether the passage's topic is related to the fact: imagine adding a sentence that states the fact to the passage. If it fits naturally, without making the passage inconsistent or illogical, answer RELATED; if it would be out of place, answer UNRELATED.
3. Your answer is YES, or NO and the answer of step 2 joined by a dash: YES, NO-RELATED or NO-UNRELATED.

== Output Format ==
Respond with ONLY one of YES, NO-RELATED, NO-UNRELATED, on a single line, without any other words.

Answer: YES

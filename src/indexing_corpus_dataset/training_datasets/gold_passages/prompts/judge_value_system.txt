You are a relevance judge. Given a fact about an entity and a passage, your task is to decide whether the passage states the fact's value for that entity and, if it does, whether the stated value is the same as the fact's value; if it does not, whether the passage's topic is related to the fact. A fact is an entity, optionally a property of it, a statement, and the value the statement gives.

== Steps ==
1. Decide whether the passage states the value of the fact for the entity: YES or NO. You only care whether the passage gives a value for what the statement describes, not which value. A value given for another entity (a similar name, a part, a neighbour) counts as NO: check from the sentence and the rest of the passage that the value is the entity's.
2-YES. If step 1 is YES, decide whether the value in the passage is the fact's value: SAME or DIFFERENT. Values match when they mean the same thing, whatever their format: numbers may differ in formatting ("1,000", "1000", "1e3") and by a small rounding difference, with the same unit when the passage gives one; dates match at the precision the fact gives (a year matches any date in that year; formats such as "January 1, 2000" and "2000-01-01" are equal); names match in any common variant ("New York City", "NYC"); a list matches when the passage gives the same items. When the statement gives a time when the value holds, the passage must give that time too.
2-NO. If step 1 is NO, decide whether the passage's topic is related to the fact: imagine adding a sentence that states the fact to the passage. If it fits naturally, without making the passage inconsistent or illogical, answer RELATED; if it would be out of place, answer UNRELATED.
3. Your answer is the answers of steps 1 and 2 joined by a dash: YES-SAME, YES-DIFFERENT, NO-RELATED or NO-UNRELATED.

== Output Format ==
Respond with ONLY one of YES-SAME, YES-DIFFERENT, NO-RELATED, NO-UNRELATED, on a single line, without any other words.

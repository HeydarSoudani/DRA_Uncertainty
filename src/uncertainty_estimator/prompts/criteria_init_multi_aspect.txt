== Rules ==
The agent will write a report that covers several aspects of a topic.
1. Every requirement stated in the request leads to at least one criterion.
2. For a broad requirement, one that a single fact cannot satisfy, list specific sub-questions instead of copying it, each one a document could answer: for example figures and trends, the groups affected, causes, responses, consequences and comparisons, as far as the requirement asks for them. A broad requirement usually yields several sub-questions; name the specific quantity, group, event or cause each one asks about.
3. Do not list background on who is asking, what the request asks to leave out, or instructions on the format or length of the report.

---example---
Example query: "I am a policy advisor for a regional water authority and need background for an upcoming hearing. I need a report on how microplastics enter freshwater ecosystems and their measured effects on the health of freshwater fish. It should also cover regulations proposed to limit microplastic pollution. I am not interested in marine ecosystems."
Example output:
{
  "criteria": [
    "the main sources and pathways by which microplastics enter freshwater ecosystems",
    "measured concentrations of microplastics in rivers and lakes",
    "the effects on fish health measured in studies of freshwater fish exposed to microplastics",
    "which freshwater fish species or life stages are most affected",
    "the regulations proposed to limit microplastic pollution and who proposed them",
    "the current status of the proposed regulations"
  ]
}

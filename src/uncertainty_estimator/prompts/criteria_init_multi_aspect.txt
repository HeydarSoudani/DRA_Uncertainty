The response to this query is a report that covers several aspects of a topic.

---rules---
4. Every requirement the query states leads to one criterion, in the query's own words as far as possible; their number has no limit. Keep a broad requirement as one criterion; do not replace it with your own sub-questions.
5. Write a limit on a requirement inside its criterion: a time, place or group the query states, or the asker's situation (such as their country) when it limits what information applies. Leave out who is asking and why.
6. Write what the query leaves out inside each criterion it narrows, as "..., not ...", never as a criterion of its own. An exception to it ("unless ...", "except ...") is its own criterion.
7. After the query's criteria, add at most the given number of criteria for information a complete report needs but the query does not state, such as figures and trends, affected groups, causes, responses, consequences or comparisons, where the topic calls for them. An added criterion never asks for what the query leaves out. Add none when the query's criteria already cover the topic.

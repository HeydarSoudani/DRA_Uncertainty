The response to this query is a report that covers several aspects of a topic.

---labels---
For this query:
- A passage supports a criterion when it gives specific facts about the subject the criterion names, such as figures, named cases, findings or measures, within the limits the criterion's wording sets: a period, place, group, or what a "..., not ..." leaves out. A mere mention of the topic, or facts only outside such a limit, is not support.
- A criterion is fully covered once three documents support it, and partially covered while one or two do.

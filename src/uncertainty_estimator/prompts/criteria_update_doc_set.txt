The response to this query is a short answer computed over every member of a set, such as a count, a sum, an average, or the earliest or latest value among the members.

---labels---
For this query:
- A passage supports a criterion when it establishes a member of the set, or whether a member has the property.
- The first criterion, the complete set of members, is fully covered when the evidence establishes every member, and partially covered when it establishes some of them.
- A criterion "<member>: <property>" is fully covered when the evidence establishes whether that member has the property.
- The last criterion, "any other member: <property>", is for the members without a criterion of their own. It is fully covered when the evidence gives the property of every such member or shows there is none, and partially covered when it gives the property of some of them.
